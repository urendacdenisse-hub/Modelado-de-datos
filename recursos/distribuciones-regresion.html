Regresión lineal

Regresión lineal simple

\[ Y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_i + \varepsilon_i, \qquad \varepsilon_i \sim \mathcal N(0,\sigma^2) \]

Cantidad Expresión
Pendiente \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}_1 = \frac{\sum (x_i-\bar{x})(y_i-\bar{y})}{\sum (x_i-\bar{x})^2}\)
Intercepto \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}_0 = \bar{y}-\hat{\beta}_1\bar{x}\)
Predicción \(\displaystyle \hat{y}_0=\hat{\beta}_0+\hat{\beta}_1x_0\)
Residuos \(\displaystyle e_i=y_i-\hat{y}_i\)
RSS \(\displaystyle \text{RSS}=\sum e_i^2\)

Distribuciones de los estimadores

\[ \hat{\beta}_1 \sim \mathcal N\left(\beta_1,\frac{\sigma^2}{S_{xx}}\right), \quad \hat{\beta}_0 \sim \mathcal N\left(\beta_0,\sigma^2\left[\frac{1}{n}+\frac{\bar{x}^2}{S_{xx}}\right]\right) \] donde \[ S_{xx}=\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i-\bar{x})^2 \]

Cantidad \(\sigma^2\) conocida \(\sigma^2\) desconocida
Estadístico para \(\beta_1\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\beta}_1-\beta_1}{\sigma/\sqrt{S_{xx}}}\sim \mathcal N(0,1)\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\beta}_1-\beta_1}{s/\sqrt{S_{xx}}}\sim t_{n-2}\)
Estadístico para \(\beta_0\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\beta}_0-\beta_0}{\sigma\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{\bar{x}^2}{S_{xx}}}}\sim \mathcal N(0,1)\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\beta}_0-\beta_0}{s\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{\bar{x}^2}{S_{xx}}}}\sim t_{n-2}\)
IC para \(\beta_1\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}_1 \pm z_{\alpha/2}\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{S_{xx}}}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}_1 \pm t_{\alpha/2,n-2}\frac{s}{\sqrt{S_{xx}}}\)
IC para \(\beta_0\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}_0 \pm z_{\alpha/2}\sigma\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{\bar{x}^2}{S_{xx}}}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}_0 \pm t_{\alpha/2,n-2}s\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{\bar{x}^2}{S_{xx}}}\)

donde \[ s^2=\frac{\text{RSS}}{n-2} \]

Distribución de la respuesta media y de una nueva observación

Para un valor dado \(x_0\):

\[ \mu_{Y|x_0}=E(Y|X=x_0)=\beta_0+\beta_1x_0 \]

y su estimación es:

\[ \hat{\mu}_{Y|x_0}=\hat{y}_0=\hat{\beta}_0+\hat{\beta}_1x_0. \]

Distribución de \(\hat{\mu}_{Y\mid x_0}\):

\[ \hat{\mu}_{Y\mid x_0}\sim \mathcal N\left(\mu_{Y\mid x_0},\sigma^2\left[\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}\right]\right) \]

Distribución de \(Y_0\): \[ Y_0\mid x_0\sim \mathcal N\left(\mu_{Y\mid x_0},\sigma^2\right) \]

Distribución del error de predicción: \[ Y_0-\hat{y}_0\sim \mathcal N\left(0,\sigma^2\left[1+\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}\right]\right) \]

Cantidad \(\sigma^2\) conocida \(\sigma^2\) desconocida
Estadístico para \(\mu_{Y\mid x_0}\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\mu}_{Y\mid x_0}-\mu_{Y\mid x_0}}{\sigma\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}}}\sim \mathcal N(0,1)\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\mu}_{Y\mid x_0}-\mu_{Y\mid x_0}}{s\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}}}\sim t_{n-2}\)
IC para \(\mu_{Y\mid x_0}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\mu}_{Y\mid x_0}\pm z_{\alpha/2}\sigma\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\mu}_{Y\mid x_0}\pm t_{\alpha/2,n-2}s\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}}\)
Estadístico para \(Y_0\) \(\displaystyle \frac{Y_0-\hat{y}_0}{\sigma\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}}}\sim \mathcal N(0,1)\) \(\displaystyle \frac{Y_0-\hat{y}_0}{s\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}}}\sim t_{n-2}\)
Intervalo de predicción para \(Y_0\) \(\displaystyle \hat{y}_0\pm z_{\alpha/2}\sigma\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{y}_0\pm t_{\alpha/2,n-2}s\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(x_0-\bar{x})^2}{S_{xx}}}\)

Regresión lineal múltiple

\[ \mathbf{Y} =\mathbf{X}{\beta} + {\varepsilon}, \qquad {\varepsilon} \sim \mathcal N_n(\mathbf{0}_n,\sigma^2I_n) \] donde $$ = \[\begin{bmatrix} 1 & x_{11} & x_{12} & \cdots & x_{1p}\\ 1 & x_{21} & x_{22} & \cdots & x_{2p}\\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\ 1 & x_{n1} & x_{n2} & \cdots & x_{np} \end{bmatrix}\]

,

\[\begin{bmatrix} \beta_0\\ \beta_1\\ \beta_2\\ \vdots\\ \beta_p \end{bmatrix}\]

$$

Cantidad Expresión
Coeficientes \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}=(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{X}^TY\)
Predicción \(\displaystyle \hat{\mathbf{Y}}=\mathbf{X}\hat{{\beta}}\)
Residuos \(\displaystyle \mathbf{e}=\mathbf{Y}-\hat{\mathbf{Y}}\)
RSS \(\displaystyle \text{RSS}=\mathbf{e}^T\mathbf{e}\)

Distribuciones de los estimadores

\[ \hat{{\beta}}\sim \mathcal N_{p+1}\left({\beta},\sigma^2(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\right), \quad \hat{\beta}_j\sim \mathcal N\left(\beta_j,\sigma^2[(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}]_{jj}\right) \]

Cantidad \(\sigma^2\) conocida \(\sigma^2\) desconocida
Error estándar de \(\hat{\beta}_j\) \(\displaystyle \sigma\sqrt{[(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}]_{jj}}\) \(\displaystyle s\sqrt{[(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}]_{jj}}\)
Estadístico \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\beta}_j-\beta_j}{\sigma\sqrt{[(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}]_{jj}}}\sim \mathcal N(0,1)\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\beta}_j-\beta_j}{s\sqrt{[(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}]_{jj}}}\sim t_{n-p-1}\)
IC para \(\beta_j\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}_j \pm z_{\alpha/2}\sigma\sqrt{[(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}]_{jj}}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\beta}_j \pm t_{\alpha/2,n-p-1}s\sqrt{[(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}]_{jj}}\)

donde \[ s^2 = \frac{\text{RSS}}{n-p-1} \]

Distribución de la respuesta media y de una nueva observación

Para un vector de predictores dado:

\[ \mathbf{x}_0= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & x_{01} & x_{02} & \cdots & x_{0p} \end{bmatrix}^T \]

la respuesta media es:

\[ \mu_{Y|\mathbf{x}_0} = E(Y|\mathbf{X}=\mathbf{x}_0) = \mathbf{x}_0^T{\beta} \]

y su estimación es:

\[ \hat{\mu}_{Y|\mathbf{x}_0} = \hat{y}_0 = \mathbf{x}_0^T\hat{{\beta}} \]

Distribución de \(\hat{\mu}_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}\) \[ \hat{\mu}_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}\sim \mathcal N\left(\mu_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0},\sigma^2\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0\right) \]

Distribución de \(Y_0\) \[ Y_0\mid\mathbf{x}_0\sim \mathcal N\left(\mu_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0},\sigma^2\right) \]

Distribución del error de predicción \[ Y_0-\hat{y}_0\sim \mathcal N\left(0,\sigma^2\left[1+\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0\right]\right) \]

Cantidad \(\sigma^2\) conocida \(\sigma^2\) desconocida
Estadístico para \(\mu_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\mu}_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}-\mu_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}}{\sigma\sqrt{\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0}}\sim \mathcal N(0,1)\) \(\displaystyle \frac{\hat{\mu}_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}-\mu_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}}{s\sqrt{\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0}}\sim t_{n-p-1}\)
IC para \(\mu_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\mu}_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}\pm z_{\alpha/2}\sigma\sqrt{\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{\mu}_{Y\mid\mathbf{x}_0}\pm t_{\alpha/2,n-p-1}s\sqrt{\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0}\)
Estadístico para \(Y_0\) \(\displaystyle \frac{Y_0-\hat{y}_0}{\sigma\sqrt{1+\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0}}\sim \mathcal N(0,1)\) \(\displaystyle \frac{Y_0-\hat{y}_0}{s\sqrt{1+\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0}}\sim t_{n-p-1}\)
Intervalo de predicción para \(Y_0\) \(\displaystyle \hat{y}_0\pm z_{\alpha/2}\sigma\sqrt{1+\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0}\) \(\displaystyle \hat{y}_0\pm t_{\alpha/2,n-p-1}s\sqrt{1+\mathbf{x}_0^T(\mathbf{X}^T\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{x}_0}\)
